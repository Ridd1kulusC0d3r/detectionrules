2. Quem é

Storm-1811 is a financially-motivated entity linked to Black Basta ransomware deployment. Storm-1811 is notable for unique phishing and social engineering mechanisms for initial access, such as overloading victim email inboxes with non-malicious spam to prompt a fake "help desk" interaction leading to the deployment of adversary tools and capabilities.

9. Hipóteses de hunting

Se Storm-1811 estiver no seu ambiente, estas são as perguntas a fazer aos seus dados (fontes de log segundo as estratégias de detecção do MITRE ATT&CK).

  1. T1566.004 Spearphishing Voice — Monitor call log records from corporate devices for unusual or unauthorized numbers, especially repeated calls to/from known malicious phone numbers. Correlate with subsequent system events (e.g., browser navigation, remote management tool execution). Dados: ApplicationLog:CallRecords (Outbound or inbound calls to high-risk or blocklisted numbers).
  2. T1667 Email Bombing — Detect abnormally high volume of inbound email messages or repetitive attachments being delivered to a single mailbox within a short time window. Defenders should look for anomalous spikes in message counts and repetitive attachment file creation events… Dados: m365:unified (Send/Receive: Unusual spikes in inbound messages to a single recipient), WinEventLog:Sysmon (EventCode=11).
  3. T1222.001 Windows Permissions — Multi-stage Windows DACL manipulation behavioral chain: (1) Process creation of permission-modifying utilities (icacls.exe, takeown.exe, attrib.exe, cacls.exe) or PowerShell ACL cmdlets, (2) Command-line analysis revealing privilege escalation intent through… Dados: WinEventLog:Security (EventCode=4688), WinEventLog:Security (EventCode=4663, 4670, 4656), WinEventLog:Security (EventCode=4663, 4656, 4658).
  4. T1056 Input Capture — Monitors for abnormal process behavior and API calls like SetWindowsHookEx, GetAsyncKeyState, or device input polling commonly used for keystroke logging. Dados: WinEventLog:Sysmon (EventCode=10), WinEventLog:Security (EventCode=4663, 4670, 4656).
  5. T1036.010 Masquerade Account Name — Detects adversary behavior where a newly created or renamed user account closely resembles existing service or administrator accounts to blend in and avoid detection. Common patterns include prefix/suffix modifications, homoglyphs, or use of names like… Dados: WinEventLog:Security (EventCode=4720), windows:osquery (User enumeration with creation/last modified timestamps).
  6. T1048.002 Exfiltration Over Asymmetric Encrypted Non-C2 Protocol — Detects non-browser processes that establish encrypted outbound connections (e.g., TLS/SSL) to unfamiliar or atypical destinations for the host/user, following a data staging or compression event. 12. Limites e confiança

  • O ATT&CK registra o que foi relatado publicamente, não tudo o que o grupo faz. Ausência de uma técnica não prova que o grupo não a usa.
  • Sobreposição de TTP não é atribuição. Muitas técnicas são compartilhadas entre grupos e com red teams. Use a assinatura comportamental como hipótese, junto com infraestrutura, malware e contexto.
  • Cobertura de detecção refere-se ao catálogo público Detection Rules, não ao seu ambiente: confira se a telemetria necessária chega ao seu SIEM.
  • Ligações marcadas como inferidas (EVTX por palavra-chave, relatórios por nome) devem ser verificadas.
